Classic Latte

Rs. 450

Product Description

Enjoy our Classic Latte, a smooth and comforting coffee made with freshly prepared espresso and perfectly steamed milk. It offers a balanced coffee flavour with a creamy texture and a light layer of milk foam.

Each cup is prepared using carefully selected coffee beans to create a rich aroma and satisfying taste. The espresso provides a bold coffee base, while the steamed milk softens its intensity and creates a smooth drinking experience.

The Classic Latte is a great choice for anyone who enjoys a milder coffee with a creamy finish. It can be enjoyed during breakfast, an afternoon break or whenever you want a warm and relaxing coffee moment.

What Makes It Special?

Our latte combines freshly brewed espresso with steamed milk in a carefully balanced ratio. The result is a comforting drink with a rich aroma, smooth consistency and gentle coffee flavour.

Every serving is freshly prepared to maintain its quality and flavour. The creamy milk texture complements the espresso and makes this coffee suitable for everyday enjoyment.

Serving Information

The Classic Latte is served fresh and warm. For the best experience, enjoy it shortly after preparation. You can select your preferred quantity using the quantity field above.

At Crimson Cup, we focus on fresh preparation, comforting flavours and enjoyable coffee moments. Our Classic Latte is one of the favourites for customers who appreciate smooth espresso-based drinks.
